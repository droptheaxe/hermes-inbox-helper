Privacy policy — Hermes Inbox Helper

Effective October 21, 2026.

Who uses this app

Hermes Inbox Helper is a personal tool used only by its owner, on his own Google account. It has no other users.

What data it accesses

With the owner's permission, it accesses his Gmail message details (such as sender, date, labels and subject), his Gmail labels and his Gmail filters. It uses this only to organise his own inbox: filing messages out of the inbox, renaming labels and creating filters he has approved.

Where data goes

Data is processed on the owner's own computer and stored there. It is not sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models. The app does not send email.

Google API Services

Hermes Inbox Helper's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

The owner can revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions and delete the locally stored data.
